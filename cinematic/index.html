Ad Studio by Brevo · Meta video ads · round 3 · cinematic

Ten fresh cinematic concepts, each built around a first frame people stop on.

No borrowed formats this time. Each concept is its own small film (a nature documentary, an autopsy, a heist, a draft night) where the story itself proves one thing the product really does. Every spoken line keeps the calm, explanatory register; every product moment uses the real Ad Studio UI; every person is generated with native audio and a natural grade.

Waiting on you: pick five

How I approached it

  • A frame you cannot scroll past. Each hook is an image that should not exist (a flock of ads, an ad in a tank, an ad on an autopsy table) paired with one line that makes you need the next sentence.
  • The genre does the explaining. Documentaries, forensic dramas and broadcasts already have a calm narrator who explains, so the professor register feels native instead of salesy.
  • The twist is the product. In every concept the turn of the story is a real feature: the survivors are what Ad Radar finds, the skeleton is what Clone keeps, the draft picks are the agent’s casting, "it was never locked" is why Radar matters.
  • Built to be shared. Each has one quotable line ("Time of death: day eleven", "There is no salary cap", "I already did") and a reason to rewatch.
  • Balanced across your two test pages: 11 to 15 lead to Winners (Ad Radar and Clone), 16 to 20 lead to the Agent page (AI creators and the agent). Production of the current five is running in the background and is not affected.
