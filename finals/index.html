01 · The 2-Year Ad

A calm host stands at the foot of a skincare ad that has been running for two years and explains why that single number tells you more than any design brief. The camera flies down a tunnel of ads that died within weeks, stops at the one that never did, and Ad Studio rebuilds it around a sunscreen.

Production notes, checks and flaws

c01 "The 2-Year Ad" · production notes

Top flags for Farooq (read first)

  • Takes were generated as 5 units (one 25 s dark-stage take plus four short cream-stage takes, brand and CTA lines in their own short units) because that was the production brief when they were generated.   • U4 needed its one allowed regeneration: take 1 said "rebuild-builds" (whisper and Gemini both heard it). Take 2 is clean. No unit has more than 2 generations.

Final: final/c01_the-2-year-ad_v1.mp4 (55.6 s, see QA below) and final/c01_the-2-year-ad_v1_share.mp4. Lands on LP A Winners (https://brevo-ads-lp.pages.dev/winners/), CTA "Find my winning ads", offer "3 free ads, no credit card".

How it was made

  • Storyboard: storyboards/c01/ (frames, PLAN.md, animatic). Production kept its shot order and signature moments: giant LED-wall ad with ACTIVE · 2 YEARS chip and 730-day odometer, rack focus, Time Tunnel (3D corridor of real Radar ads folding grey, floor day markers, day rail), brake at DAY 730 with the single survivor, 3D flip to "That’s not an old ad. That’s a tested idea." with Nadia tapping it, cream-stage close-up, carry-across of Nadia into a picture-in-picture, survivor cards assembling into the real Ad Radar grid, spring filter chips, real detail panel with cursor and click, Clone Morph (scan line, zones, Acne flips out, White cast lands, tins dissolve into particles that form the Glowday tube), split RUNNING 2 YEARS vs YOUR VERSION, mosaic end card with the real Brevo wordmark + Ad Studio lockup and CTA.
  • Takes: Seedance 2.5 face via treg, 1080p, native audio, no music, first frames published on adstudio-refs (c01/u1_dark_full.jpg cropped from storyboard keyframe k1; c01/u2_cream_mcu.jpg new Nano Banana Pro keyframe with headroom for captions). U1 was matted with hyperframes remove-background (u2net human) so the giant ad, the tunnel and the flip card sit behind and beside her.
  • Film: HyperFrames 0.8.206, one root composition generated by make.py (styles in style.css, word timings in qa/*.words.json, cut points in timing.json). Rendered with the playwright chrome-headless-shell (PRODUCER_HEADLESS_SHELL_PATH) because the system Chrome launch times out on this machine.
  • Real product UI only: Ad Radar grid frame from the 2x recording J2_radar_to_clone (t = 50 s), detail panel and header cropped from J2_detail_2x.png. Radar brands shown as they are. All prices on screen are USD ($34.00).
  • Word timings: the whisper word stamps ran up to 1.4 s early, so align.py places the exact script words on each take's own speech runs (silencedetect) by syllable rate. Captions, pills, the odometer landing, the 730 badge, White cast, the chip stack and the offer stickers are keyed to those times.
  • Sound (STANDARDS.md): voice from the takes at full level; music is one ElevenLabs instrumental (55.6 s, real ending under the end card) at gain 0.18 on a bus at x0.67 with a x0.90 dip on every spoken phrase (merged under 0.3 s, 0.12 s cosine attack, 0.35 s release), 0.4 s fade-in; 8 quiet SFX only on meaningful events (chip lands, flip reveal, tap, 730 badge lands, two clicks, clone locks, CTA lands), none on a brand word or key line, no impacts, risers, shimmer or bass hits. Static gain to -16 LUFS, limiter at -1 dB (remix_c01.py).

Flaws and taste notes (timestamps in the final)

  • 0.0 to 3.5 s: a faint ghost of the LED wall's headline ("Our return policy is simple") shows through the dark scrim behind the first caption while the camera dollies in. Readable, but not fully clean.
  • 0.0 to 9.4 s: Nadia is matted (u2net human matte) onto the stage in front of the giant ad. No halo is visible in the 2 fps scan; worth one look at full size around her hair at 4 to 9 s.
  • 9.45 s: the move into the Time Tunnel is a zoom-through past her (scale and fade), not the 3-frame whip streak from the storyboard.
  • 12.3 s: the red "STOPPED · DAY 26" stamp on a falling card illustrates the claim "most ads are switched off within a few weeks". It is not a Radar number. The day rail and floor markers (DAY 1 to 730) are also illustrative, tied to the camera's travel.
  • 21.0 to 23.5 s (tap): in the take she raises her finger beside the card's right edge rather than onto its face. The ripple sits at her fingertip, so it reads as a tap at the edge. She also glances back to camera mid-tap, which is natural.
  • 24.07 s: a hard cut from the dark stage to the cream stage (the luma jump flagged by QA is this deliberate cut). 37.33 s: the white detail panel slides in fast over the dark grid (also deliberate).
  • 28.95 to 29.65 s: during the carry-across, a circular mask shrinks the cream plate into the picture-in-picture. For about 0.3 s it reads as a rounded cream shape.
  • 29.4 to 48.2 s: lines 3 to 5 (U3 to U5) play in a 215 px circular picture-in-picture under the UI and morph scenes. Lip sync is native, but her face is small there.
  • The storyboard's 118 and 241 day badges were dropped because they are not verified Radar values. Only the captured 730 days / 2 years appears as a number, and the other cards get the real "Long-running" signal label.
  • 49.2 to 55.6 s: the end card holds 6.4 s after the last word, so the music's real ending lands under it. The mosaic keeps turning, and the CTA pops and pulses once. Trim the tail if it feels long; music and picture end together at 55.6 s.
  • Music is one continuous 55.6 s ElevenLabs piece matched to the film length (no stitches), so its accents were not re-aligned to individual picture cuts.
  • STANDARDS.md says long takes with the brand and CTA lines inside them. These takes predate that rule (see the top flags).

QA (final file)

  • Specs: 1080x1920, 30 fps, H.264 High, AAC 48 kHz, 55.6 s, 49.0 MB. The share copy is 28.0 MB (2-pass 3.9 Mbps), 55.6 s, -16.1 LUFS.
  • Loudness: -16.1 LUFS integrated, true peak -1.0 dBFS (static gain plus limiter, no dynamic loudnorm).
  • Words: whisper on the isolated voice stem matches the full script word for word, in order (difflib diff empty, with "Bravo" read as Brevo). The Gemini 2.5 Pro listening check also found a perfect match, Brevo said BRAY-voh with no pause, Ad Studio heard as "ad studio", no glitches or cut words: VERDICT PASS (qa/final_qa.json).
  • Brevo formant check on U3: the first vowel's F2 peaks at 2,619 Hz (BRAY), and there is no silence inside the word.
  • Frame scan at 2 fps (qa/scan_sheet_1..4.jpg) checked for: no black frames, no flash frames except the two deliberate cuts above, no green cast on footage (natural grade), the same face and linen shirt in every take, five fingers on the tap hand, no garbled product label (Glowday tube is the real clone output), no captions over her face, no non-USD prices ($34.00 only), no "Shopify" or "Klaviyo" anywhere, no em or en dashes on screen.
  • Safe zones: captions sit at 270 to 480 px. The picture-in-picture and stickers sit above 1640 px. The end card CTA is at 1060 to 1190 px.
