01 · The 2-Year Ad

A calm host stands at the foot of a skincare ad that has been running for two years and explains why that single number tells you more than any design brief. The camera flies down a tunnel of ads that died within weeks, stops at the one that never did, and Ad Studio rebuilds it around a sunscreen.

Animatic: style frames on the planned timing with scratch narration. Final voices are native audio from the generated takes; final motion is fully animated.

Locked script

Host (Nadia)0-28 s

This skincare ad has been running on Instagram for two years. That detail matters more than the ad itself. Most ads are switched off within a few weeks, because they stop paying for themselves. So when a brand keeps paying for one ad for two years, it is telling you, quite clearly, that the idea works.

Host (Nadia)28-55 s

Ad Studio by Brevo tracks these ads from the day they launch, and sorts them by how long they have been running. You pick a proven winner, and it rebuilds the same idea around your product, your brand and your offer. You can start with three free ads, and no credit card.

Every ad that keeps running for months has two parts. There is the surface, which is the brand, the product and the colors.
